# Returns & Refunds Policy

This policy covers when to accept a returned item and how the refund is paid.

## Scope
This policy applies to items sold by NovaMart in NovaMart stores and on novamart.com. Employee-discount purchases follow this policy with the additions in POL-EMP-004; defective, damaged and recalled items are handled under POL-DMG-002 and exchanges under POL-EXC-003.

## Standard return window
Customers may return most items within 30 days of the receipt date. The item must be unused, in its original packaging and with all tags attached.

## Electronics
Electronics (laptops, tablets, phones, TVs, headphones and other audio, cameras and games consoles) must be returned within 14 days of the receipt date. Opened electronics are accepted within that window if the item is complete and undamaged with all accessories; a 15% restocking fee is deducted from the refund. The fee is waived only when the item is defective (see POL-DMG-002).

## Refunds with a receipt
Refund to the original payment method. Cash purchases are refunded in cash up to $100; above that, by NovaMart e-gift card or cheque within 5 business days. Card refunds post back to the card in 3–5 business days. Gift-card purchases are refunded to a NovaMart e-gift card (see POL-GC-006).

## Returns without a receipt
Without a receipt, offer store credit only, at the item's lowest selling price in the last 60 days. Ask for a government photo ID and record it on the return. No-receipt returns are capped at $150 per customer in any rolling 30-day period. Electronics cannot be returned without a receipt.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, perishable food, opened personal-care products, custom-cut items and anything marked Final Sale cannot be returned.

## Approvals
A Store Manager must approve any return over $500 and any return outside the windows above. If in doubt, ask the Manager on Duty.
