# Employee Discount

This policy explains who can use the employee discount, what it covers and how returns of discounted items work.

## Discount rates
Employees receive 20% off regular-price merchandise and 10% off electronics. The discount is applied at the till to the regular shelf price and cannot be applied after the sale has been completed.

## Eligibility
The discount is available to employees who have completed 30 days' service, and to one household member registered with the People Team. Show your staff ID and quote your employee number at the till for every discounted purchase.

## Exclusions
The discount does not apply to gift cards, prepaid cards, lottery products, services such as delivery and installation, or clearance items. It cannot be combined with coupons or with a price match (see POL-EXC-003).

## Returning discounted items
Items bought with the employee discount follow the standard return windows in POL-RET-001. The refund is the discounted price actually paid, never the full shelf price, and it goes back to the original payment method. Restocking fees for opened electronics apply exactly as they do for any customer.

## Misuse
Using the discount for resale, sharing your employee number, or buying for people outside your registered household is misuse and leads to disciplinary action. Loss Prevention reviews the discount logs every month and reports anomalies to the People Team.
