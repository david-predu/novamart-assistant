# Escalation Contacts

Who to contact when a situation is not covered by these policies, or when a policy tells you to escalate.

## Manager on Duty (MOD)
Ext. 200 or the duty phone. The Manager on Duty is your first stop for anything not covered by these policies: customer disputes, safety concerns, staffing and any exception you are unsure about.

## Store Manager
Approves returns over $500, returns outside the return window and any other exception to policy. Ask the Manager on Duty to contact the Store Manager if they are not on the floor.

## Customer Care
Ext. 4500 or care@novamart.example, open 8am to 8pm every day. Contact Customer Care for online orders, delivery damage, lost parcels, carrier traces, e-gift card refunds and gift card replacements.

## Loss Prevention
Ext. 4800. Contact Loss Prevention for suspected fraud, gift card scams, discount misuse and theft.

## Safety hotline
0800-555-0199, available 24/7. Use it for injuries, hazards and product safety concerns.

## HR Service Centre
hr@novamart.example. Contact the HR Service Centre for pay, benefits, leave and other employment questions.

## IT Service Desk
Ext. 4900. Contact the IT Service Desk for till faults, Staff app problems and order system access.

## When these policies do not answer
If these policies do not answer a question, do not guess — ask the Manager on Duty (MOD) or the team listed above.
