# Exchanges & Price Match

This policy covers swapping an item for another and matching a competitor's price.

## Exchange windows
Exchanges follow the same windows as returns in POL-RET-001 (30-day standard, 14-day electronics). The item must meet the same condition requirements as a return.

## Even exchanges without a receipt
An even exchange (the same item in a different size or colour) can be done without a receipt as long as the NovaMart tag is still attached and the item is unused.

## Price differences on exchange
If the replacement is cheaper, refund the difference to the original payment method. If it is dearer, the customer pays the difference at the till. Restocking fees for opened electronics still apply as in POL-RET-001.

## Price match
NovaMart matches a lower price from Target, Walmart, Best Buy or Amazon (only items sold by Amazon itself). The item must be identical (brand, model, colour and size), in stock at the competitor and sold by the competitor itself. The customer must show the live price on the competitor's website or in its current advertisement at the time of purchase.

## Price adjustment
If NovaMart lowers its own price on an item within 10 days of purchase, refund the difference on presentation of the receipt.

## Exclusions
No price match or price adjustment during Black Friday week (24–30 November) or on Cyber Monday, and none on clearance items, bundles, or purchases made with a coupon or the employee discount (see POL-EMP-004).

## Approvals
Any price match with a difference over $50 needs approval from the Manager on Duty before it is applied.
