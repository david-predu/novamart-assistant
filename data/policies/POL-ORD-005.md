# Orders, Shipping & Delivery

This policy explains how to look up an online order, what each status means and what to tell customers about delivery.

## Order numbers
Every online order has a number in the form NM- followed by five digits, for example NM-10250. Ask the customer for the full number and look it up in the order system before you answer any question about the order.

## Order status meanings
- PROCESSING: paid and confirmed, not yet picked; can still be cancelled at the till or by Customer Care.
- PICKED: being packed in the warehouse; can no longer be cancelled.
- SHIPPED: handed to the carrier; a tracking number is available.
- OUT_FOR_DELIVERY: on the carrier's vehicle for delivery today.
- DELIVERED: the carrier has confirmed delivery.
- DELAYED: a carrier exception has occurred; the order system shows a revised ETA.
- CANCELLED: cancelled before shipping; any payment has been refunded.
- RETURNED: the item has come back to NovaMart and the refund has been issued.

## Shipping options
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days and is free on orders over $50. Express shipping takes 1–2 business days and is charged at the rate shown at checkout.

## Delayed orders
When an order is DELAYED, apologise for the inconvenience and share the revised ETA (estimated delivery date) exactly as shown in the order system. Never promise a delivery date that is not in the order system. If the order is more than 5 business days past its original ETA, offer a refund of the shipping fee; Customer Care processes that refund.

## Lost in transit
If a SHIPPED order shows no carrier movement for 10 business days, treat it as lost in transit. Contact Customer Care, who open a carrier trace and arrange a replacement or refund.
