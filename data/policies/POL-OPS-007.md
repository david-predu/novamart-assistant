# Attendance & Peak-Season Operations

This policy sets out how to report an absence and the stricter rules that apply during peak season.

## Reporting an absence
If you cannot work a scheduled shift, notify the Manager on Duty at least 2 hours before the shift starts, either through the Staff app or by phone.

## Sick notes
A doctor's note is not required for one or two consecutive days of absence. It is required from the 3rd consecutive day and must be handed to the Manager on Duty on your return.

## Unreported absences
An absence that is not reported before the shift starts is recorded as unreported. Three unreported absences in any 6-month period result in a written warning.

## Peak season
Peak season runs from 20 November to 31 December. Time-off requests for this period are blocked, and every employee is scheduled to work Black Friday and the weekend that follows it.

## Peak-season absence override
During peak season the standard rules above are replaced by the following. You must give notice at least 4 hours before the shift AND by a phone call to the Manager on Duty; a message in the Staff app alone is not sufficient. A doctor's note is required from the 2nd consecutive day of absence. The Manager on Duty backfills the shift from the on-call list.

## Shift swaps during peak season
Shift swaps during peak season are allowed only with the Manager on Duty's approval, requested at least 24 h before the earlier of the two shifts.
