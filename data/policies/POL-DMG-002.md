# Damaged, Defective & Recalled Items

This policy covers items that do not work, arrived damaged or have been recalled. It takes priority over the standard windows in POL-RET-001.

## Defective items
A customer may return a defective item within 90 days of the purchase date, whatever the category, including electronics. This 90-day window replaces the 30-day and 14-day windows in POL-RET-001. No restocking fee applies. The customer must show the receipt or give the order number so you can confirm the purchase date. Offer a replacement first; if none is available, refund to the original payment method.

## Manufacturer warranty
Once the 90-day window has passed, faults are covered by the manufacturer's warranty. Help the customer find the warranty contact on the packaging or in the manual; do not process a return or refund in store.

## Damaged on arrival
If an online order arrives damaged, the customer must report it to Customer Care within 7 days of delivery, with photos of the item and the packaging. Customer Care arranges a free replacement or a full refund. If the customer comes into a store instead, take the order number and the photos and pass them to Customer Care.

## Recalled items
A recalled item is refunded in full at any time, with or without a receipt. Remove any remaining stock from the shelf, log the return in the Recall Tracker and follow the recall notice for disposal.

## Suspected safety hazard
If an item is reported to smoke, spark, overheat, leak or cause an injury, stop selling it immediately, tell the Manager on Duty and call the Safety hotline (see POL-ESC-009). Keep the item and its packaging for inspection.
