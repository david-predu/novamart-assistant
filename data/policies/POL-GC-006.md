# Gift Cards

This policy covers the sale, redemption and replacement of NovaMart gift cards and e-gift cards.

## Returns and cash redemption
Gift cards are non-returnable and non-refundable, and they cannot be redeemed for cash. The only exception is where state law requires cash-back of a small remaining balance; if a customer asks for this, check with the Manager on Duty before paying anything out.

## Expiry and fees
NovaMart gift cards never expire and carry no dormancy or maintenance fees.

## Lost or stolen cards
A lost or stolen gift card is replaced only when the customer can show the original purchase receipt and give the card number. Take these details and pass them to Customer Care, who cancel the old card and issue the replacement.

## Buying gift cards
Gift cards cannot be bought with the employee discount (see POL-EMP-004) or paid for with another gift card.

## Refunds for purchases paid with a gift card
When a customer returns an item that was paid for with a gift card, in full or in part, the refund for that portion is issued to a NovaMart e-gift card. It is never paid in cash, even if the customer has lost the original card.

## Scam warning signs
Be alert to a customer buying several high-value gift cards while on the phone, reading card numbers to someone, or saying they were told to buy the cards to pay a bill or a fine. Pause the sale politely and alert the Manager on Duty or Loss Prevention (see POL-ESC-009).
