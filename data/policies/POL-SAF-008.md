# Loading Dock Safety & PPE

This policy lists the protective equipment and working rules for everyone on the loading dock.

## Required PPE
On the loading dock you must wear safety-toe boots and a high-visibility vest at all times. Wear cut-resistant gloves whenever you handle strapping or use a blade. A hard hat is required while the dock leveller is in use or when anyone is working on racking above 2 m.

## Equipment operation
Only certified operators may drive the pallet jack or the forklift. Certification must be renewed every 2 years; the Manager on Duty keeps the list of current operators. Never ride on a pallet or on the forks.

## Manual handling
The maximum solo lift is 20 kg. Anything heavier is a team lift or is moved with equipment. Keep the load close to your body, bend at the knees and clear the path before you lift.

## Spills and hazards
Cordon off any spill, damaged racking or blocked fire exit straight away and report it to the Manager on Duty. Do not move a damaged pallet until it has been checked.

## Injuries
If someone is injured, call the store first aider immediately and then the Safety hotline (see POL-ESC-009). An incident form must be completed within 24 h of the injury, however minor it seems.
